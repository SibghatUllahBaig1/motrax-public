## 🎯 The problem

Car enthusiasts are scattered across general social networks, forums and marketplaces. Their cars, build histories, local events and parts deals live in different places — and none of them treat the **vehicle** as the centre of the profile.

## 💡 The solution

**MotraX** puts the garage first. Every member has a profile and a virtual garage of vehicles, a social feed to share builds, a map of local car meets, direct messaging and a marketplace — one app built around the car community.

---

## ✨ Key features

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🚗 **Garage & vehicle profiles** | Showcase each vehicle with its own profile page and build details. |
| 📰 **Home feed & posts** | Share photos and updates, like and comment on community posts. |
| 📍 **Local car meets & maps** | Discover and join nearby meets on an interactive map. |
| 💬 **Chat** | Message other enthusiasts directly. |
| 🛒 **Marketplace** | Buy and sell cars and parts inside the community. |
| 🔎 **Search & explore** | Find people, vehicles, meets and listings. |
| 👤 **Account types** | Separate onboarding for different kinds of users (e.g. individual enthusiasts vs. other account types). |
| 🔔 **Notifications & settings** | Activity alerts and account preferences. |

---

## 🏗️ Engineering highlights

- **Feature-first, modular Flutter architecture** — 25+ self-contained feature modules (`garage · home_feed · meets · maps · marketplace · chat · vehicle_profile · search · onboarding …`).
- **GetX** for state management and route management, with a centralised routes layer.
- **Reusable component library** — shared UI components keep the app visually consistent and quick to extend.
- **Polished, brand-led UI** — custom typography, iconography and cached network imagery for smooth scrolling feeds.
- **Backend-ready structure** — a dedicated `data` layer so the UI can be connected to any API or Firebase backend.

## 🧰 Tech stack

**Flutter · Dart · GetX · Google Fonts · cached_network_image · Font Awesome · Shared Preferences**
